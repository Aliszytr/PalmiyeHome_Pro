A whole apartment, not just a room

Palmiye Home is a bright 1+1 apartment: a living room with an open kitchen, a separate bedroom and a bathroom with a washing machine.

Light floors, soft daylight and simple, comfortable furniture. There is room to cook, to unpack properly and to rest after a day at the beach or in the mountains.

  • Living room with sofa and Smart TV
  • Open kitchen with fridge, oven and cooktop
  • Bedroom with a double bed
  • Bathroom with shower and washing machine

Have breakfast at your own table, put a wash on, take an afternoon swim and come back to a quiet apartment in the evening. Palmiye Home is made for staying, not just sleeping — for couples, small families and anyone who prefers a home to a hotel room.

Questions & answers

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is between 14:00 and 23:00. Check-out is by 11:00.

How many guests can stay?

The apartment is suitable for up to 4 guests.

When is the pool open?

The shared pool is seasonal and has an adult and a children's section. Opening dates can change each year, so please ask when you check your dates.

Is there parking?

Yes, free open-air parking is available on site.

Is there a baby cot or high chair?

Yes, a baby cot and a high chair can be provided on request.

How far is the beach?

The beach is about 2 km away — roughly 15–20 min on foot. Kemer centre is about 10 minutes by car.

Is the calendar on this site final?

No. The calendar is updated manually and is only indicative. Your dates are confirmed by the host on WhatsApp or by Booking.com / Airbnb when you book there.
