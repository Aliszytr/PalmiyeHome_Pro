A whole apartment, not just a room

Palmiye Home is a bright 1+1 apartment: a living room with an open kitchen, a separate bedroom and a bathroom with a washing machine.

Light floors, soft daylight and simple, comfortable furniture. There is room to cook, to unpack properly and to rest after a day at the beach or in the mountains.

  • Living room with sofa and Smart TV
  • Open kitchen with fridge, oven and cooktop
  • Bedroom with a double bed
  • Bathroom with shower and washing machine

Your holiday home

Have breakfast at your own table, put a wash on, take an afternoon swim and come back to a quiet apartment in the evening. Palmiye Home is made for staying, not just sleeping — for couples, small families and anyone who prefers a home to a hotel room.

  • Your own kitchen and living space
  • Quiet residential setting at the foot of the mountains
  • Easy to reach Kemer, the beach and Göynük Canyon

Pool & outdoor

The seasonal shared pool has an adult and a children's section, sun loungers and a view of the mountains. Around it: lawns, palms and garden seating where the day slows down.

Seasonal outdoor pool. The opening time can vary with maintenance and weather. For stays before 1 June, we recommend checking with us whether the pool is in use before you book. The apartment itself can be booked all year round.

Questions & answers

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is between 14:00 and 23:00. Check-out is by 11:00.

How many guests can stay?

The apartment is suitable for up to 4 guests.

When is the pool open?

The shared pool has an adult and a children's section. Seasonal outdoor pool. The opening time can vary with maintenance and weather. For stays before 1 June, we recommend checking with us whether the pool is in use before you book. The apartment itself can be booked all year round.

Is there parking?

Yes, free open-air parking is available on site.

Can I book from within Turkey?

Yes — on WhatsApp or on Airbnb. Booking.com currently does not offer bookings for properties in Turkey to users located in Turkey.

Is there a baby cot or high chair?

Yes, a baby cot and a high chair can be provided on request.

How far is the beach?

The beach is about 2 km away — roughly 15–20 min on foot. Kemer centre is about 10 minutes by car.

How up to date is the calendar?

The calendar is synchronised automatically from our Booking.com and Airbnb calendars; bookings made directly with us are blocked there too. Final availability is confirmed when you book — by the host on WhatsApp or by Booking.com / Airbnb.
