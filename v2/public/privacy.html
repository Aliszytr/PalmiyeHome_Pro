Privacy policy

Technical description last updated: 2026-10-05

Who we are

This website belongs to Palmiye Home, a holiday apartment in Göynük, Kemer, Antalya, Turkey. Contact: aliszytr@gmail.com.

Cookies and your choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether Google Analytics and Google Ads cookies may be used. Until you accept, Google consent is set to “denied” and these cookies are not stored. Your choice is saved in your browser (localStorage key “palmiye_cookies”) so the banner does not appear again; you can change it any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of each page. Your language choice is saved in the same way (key “palmiye_lang”).

Google Analytics and Google Ads

The site loads Google's tag (gtag.js) for Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads conversion measurement. With consent denied, Google may still receive cookieless measurement signals (for example that a page was viewed or a booking link was clicked) without storing cookies on your device. With consent granted, Google may use cookies to measure visits and ad performance. We record clicks on WhatsApp, Booking.com and Airbnb links and the use of the availability calendar; we do not send names, phone numbers, email addresses or chat contents to Google.

Visit notifications

When a page is opened, the site sends our own server a short anonymous notice (language, a rough device type such as phone or desktop, the page path and the name of the referring website). Our server forwards a short message to the owner's Telegram account. Your IP address, browser details and screen size are not included in the message and no per-visitor record is kept; only a shared counter used to limit the number of notifications is stored. Within the same browser session the notice is sent once (sessionStorage key “palmiye_notified”).

Map

The Google Maps map is not loaded automatically. It loads only if you click “Show map”; Google then processes your request under its own privacy policy.

Booking platforms and WhatsApp

Links to WhatsApp, Booking.com and Airbnb open those services. Anything you send or book there is handled by those services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us about the data we hold or request its deletion by writing to aliszytr@gmail.com.
